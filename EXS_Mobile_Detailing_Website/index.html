Our Story

Built for Kilmore,
loud on flavour.

Mad Buns sits inside Kilmore Village Shopping Centre behind a hand-painted mural and a wall of hot pink you can spot from the car park. It's the kind of place we wanted for our own town — no fuss, proper portions, and a menu built around burgers that actually taste like someone was paying attention. Whether you're grabbing a quick lunch or feeding the whole footy team after training, we cook it the same way: fresh, fast, and a little bit mad.

Fresh, made-to-order

Every burger built when you order it — nothing sitting under a heat lamp.

Vegetarian options

Plant-based patties and loaded veg sides for anyone skipping the meat.

Kids' menu

Sorted portions and simple options for the little ones.

Uber Eats delivery

Can't make it in? We deliver across Kilmore and Wallan.
